## 1st place, LA MUESTRA innovation fair, 2022

**Universidad de los Andes, Faculty of Engineering, Innovation Week (Semana de la Innovación)**, November 25, 2022

[LiteContract]({% link _projects/10_litecontract.md %}) took first place among all Systems and Computing Engineering projects. It's a web app that reads an employment contract and explains its key terms in plain language.
